ABOUT PRECISION BURN STUDIO

Meet Gregory.
The owner & creator.

One customer. One idea. One creation at a time.

My name is Gregory, and I’m the owner and creator of Precision Burn Studio.

Starting this business means a lot to me. I didn’t start Precision Burn Studio simply because I wanted to sell engraved products. I started it because I wanted to create something of my own—something built around creativity, craftsmanship, and the ability to take an idea someone has in their mind and turn it into something real.

I’ve always enjoyed working with my hands, figuring out how things work, and taking on projects that allow me to be creative. When I discovered laser engraving, I saw an opportunity to bring those things together and create something special.

What started as an idea has grown into a business built around bringing people's ideas to life.

More Than Just an Engraving

At Precision Burn Studio, I don’t believe in simply choosing something off a shelf and putting a name on it. I want to work with my customers to create something that is truly personal to them.

Sometimes a customer comes to me with a complete design. Other times, they may only have a picture, a thought, or a simple idea. That’s where I come in.

My job is to take that vision and turn it into something you can actually hold in your hands.

Whether it’s a personalized gift, a business item, a special keepsake, a memorial piece, something for an event, or simply something you’ve always wanted made, your idea matters to me.

Our Mission

My mission with Precision Burn Studio is to provide personalized, high-quality craftsmanship while giving every customer the opportunity to bring their own ideas to life.

I want every piece I create to have meaning behind it. I want my customers to look at their finished product and know that it was made specifically for them—not something mass-produced and simply picked off a shelf.

I’m building Precision Burn Studio one customer, one idea, and one creation at a time.

The Heart of Precision Burn Studio

My goal is not only to create great products, but to build relationships with the people who trust me with their ideas.

When you bring an idea to Precision Burn Studio, you’re not just placing an order. You’re starting a conversation about what you envision, what you want to create, and how we can turn that vision into something real.

FAQ

Questions? Start here.

Can I send you a picture of what I want?

Yes. The custom-order form is designed for customers to upload pictures, artwork, logos and reference images.

Do I need a finished design?

No. You can submit an idea or reference and explain what you're looking for. Gregory can review it and discuss what may be possible.

How is pricing determined?

Custom pricing can depend on the item, material, size, quantity, artwork and amount of detail. A quote is provided before production begins.

Can you do business or bulk orders?

Yes. Business products, event items, recognition pieces and other bulk projects can be submitted through the custom-order form.

What if I'm not sure what material or product I need?

That's okay. Tell us what you are trying to create, and we can start the conversation from there.
